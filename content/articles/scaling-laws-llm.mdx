In deep learning, performance scales predictably with model size, dataset size, and compute budget. These relations are known as **scaling laws**.

## Power-Law Scaling

Kaplan et al. (2020) demonstrated that cross-entropy loss $L$ scales as a power law with compute $C$, dataset size $D$, and parameters $N$:

$$
L(N) \approx \left(\frac{N_c}{N}\right)^{\alpha_N}
$$

As parameters increase, we observe **emergent capabilities**—tasks like arithmetic, reasoning, or translation that are impossible for small models but arise suddenly at specific compute thresholds.
