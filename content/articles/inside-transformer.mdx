The Transformer architecture, introduced by Vaswani et al. in 2017, has revolutionized natural language processing and deep learning. At its core is the **self-attention mechanism**.

## Self-Attention Mechanism

Attention allows a model to focus on different parts of the input sequence when producing an output. For a query $Q$, key $K$, and value $V$, scaled dot-product attention is defined as:

$$
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

Where $d_k$ is the dimensionality of the keys. By stacking multiple attention heads, the model learns rich relationships across different representation subspaces.
